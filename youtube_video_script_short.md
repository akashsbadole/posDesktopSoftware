## Production Notes

### Editing Style
- **Cuts**: Every 2-3 seconds (fast montage)
- **Transitions**: Quick cuts, occasional zoom effects
- **Text**: Bold, large font (impactful), animated in
- **Sound Effects**: 
  - Scanner beep
  - Cash register cha-ching
  - Notification ding (KDS)
  - Print receipt whir
  - Whoosh for transitions
- **Music**: Upbeat electronic/pop track (instrumental only for voiceover clarity)
- **Color Palette**: Brand colors: Blue (#1E40AF), Orange (#F97316), White, dark background (#0F172A for contrast)

### Shooting Tips
- Record screen at 1920×1080 or higher
- Use dark theme in app for video
- Clean desktop before recording
- Use "Sample Data" preset for demo
- Rehearse timing to hit exact scene boundaries

### Captions
- Burn in subtitles (bold white text with black outline)
- Auto-captions as backup (YouTube auto-generated)

### Thumbnail
- Dashboard screenshot with bold text: "FREE POS (No Monthly Fees!)"
- Or: Split screen - left shows competitor prices ($), right shows "₹0"

### Video Title Options
1. "Free Restaurant POS System That Works Offline"
2. "I Built a Completely Free POS (Here's Why)"  
3. "Stop Paying for POS - Use This Instead"
4. "POS Billing - 100% Free, All Features Included"
5. "The Only Free POS You'll Ever Need"

## Platform-Specific Notes

### YouTube (Main)
- Full 3-minute version
- End screen with subscribe button
- Description: Download link, GitHub link, feature timestamps
- Chapters: Use scene titles as chapter markers

### Instagram Reels
- 60-90 second cutdown
- Vertical (9:16 aspect ratio)
- Text covers top/bottom (avoid center for faces)
- Loop-friendly ending

### TikTok
- 60 seconds max
- Trending audio track (use original voiceover or popular business sound)
- Hashtags: #restaurant #pos #free #smallbusiness #technology

### Facebook
- Full 3-minute version (square or 16:9)
- Direct download link in post
- Pin comment with link

### LinkedIn
- Trim to 2 minutes, professional tone
- Focus on business value: ROI, cost savings, efficiency
- Tag industry pages: Restaurant, Small Business, Tech
