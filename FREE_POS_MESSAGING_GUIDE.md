- ✅ Completely free (Square charges per transaction)
- ✅ Offline-first (Square requires internet)
- ✅ Unlimited stores (Square charges per location)
- ✅ No per-user fees (Square charges per user)
- ✅ All features included (Square has limited free tier)

- ✅ Completely free (Toast charges subscription)

- ✅ Unlimited stores (Toast charges per location)

### vs. Vend:
- ✅ Completely free (Vend charges subscription)

## Key Takeaways

1. **Always emphasize "completely free"** - This is your biggest differentiator
2. **Never mention premium tiers** - You don't have them
3. **List all features as included** - Everything is free
4. **Use consistent messaging** - Repeat the same key phrases
5. **Focus on value** - Show what they get for free
6. **Compare to competitors** - Highlight the cost savings
7. **Build trust** - Explain why it's free (open-source, community-driven)
8. **Make CTAs clear** - "Download free" not "Start trial"
