## Key Takeaways

### Always Use:
✅ "Completely free"
✅ "No subscription"
✅ "No hidden fees"
✅ "Forever"
✅ "All features"
✅ "Unlimited"

### Never Use:
❌ "Free trial"
❌ "Premium tier"
❌ "Upgrade"
❌ "Limited time"
❌ "Freemium"
❌ "Pro version"

### Tone:
- Confident (we're free forever)
- Clear (no confusion)
- Honest (no hidden agenda)
- Helpful (focused on user value)
- Friendly (approachable)

## Quick Reference

| Scenario | Say This | Don't Say This |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| Website | "Completely free" | "Free trial" |
| Email | "No subscriptions" | "Upgrade now" |
| Social | "All features included" | "Premium features" |
| Support | "Completely free forever" | "Limited time offer" |
| Pricing | "$0/month forever" | "$99/month premium" |
| Feature | "Included free" | "Premium only" |
| CTA | "Download free" | "Start trial" |
