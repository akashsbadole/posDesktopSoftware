Features

Everything you need to run a restaurant.

Built for speed, designed for offline use. No internet? No problem. All your data lives in a local SQLite database.

🛒

Point of Sale

Full billing with product grid, barcode scanner support, Cash/Card/UPI payments, per-item and global discounts, tax calculation, and instant receipt generation.

📋

Order Management

Complete order history with delivery tracking, order notes, refunds, cancellations with reason capture, and a full refund approval workflow.

👨‍🍳

Kitchen Display

Dedicated KDS window for kitchen staff. Item-level completion tracking, auto-refresh every 10 seconds, separate Tauri window for second screen.

👥

Customer CRM

Customer database with loyalty points, wallet system, visit tracking, spend history, and phone-based lookup at checkout.

🪑

Table & Reservations

Visual table grid with status tracking (Available/Occupied/Reserved), table booking system, and order-to-table assignment.

📊

Analytics & Reports

Real-time dashboard, hourly sales, staff performance, top products, low-stock alerts, GST reports (GSTR-1/GSTR-3B), Tally & QuickBooks export.

💰

Finance

Expense tracking with custom categories, coupon/promo system, customer wallet, day-end cash reconciliation, and full audit trail.

👤

Staff Management

PIN-based login with role-based access (Admin/Staff), attendance clock-in/out, shift scheduling, and performance tracking.

🔧

Inventory & Supply

Product catalog with stock tracking, ingredient management with recipes, supplier directory, and purchase order workflow.

🔒

Security

AES-256-GCM encryption for credentials, bcrypt PIN hashing, brute-force lockout, role-based screen access, immutable audit logs.

☁️

Cloud & LAN Sync

Push/pull to Neon PostgreSQL, LAN server for local network devices, SMS via Twilio, WhatsApp messaging integration.

⌨️

Keyboard Shortcuts

F1-F8 navigation, P for checkout, C for clear cart, 1-3 for order types, ? for help. Built for speed at the counter.
